YouTube subtitles in your language — with an optional AI voice-over

VexAI translates a video's subtitles and shows them on the player, synced with playback. Turn on the voice-over and the translated lines are read aloud in a standard synthetic voice while the original audio is lowered — like a dubbed version of the video.

Add to Chrome — it's free

Translated subtitles work without an account.

How it works

  1. Open a YouTube video that has subtitles (manual or auto-generated).
  2. Click the VexAI button on the player. The video pauses while the first lines are prepared.
  3. Watch. Translated subtitles appear in place of the originals; with voice-over on, each line is spoken in your language, timed to the video.

Details that matter

  • Synced with playback. Translated lines follow the video's own caption timing, and the video pauses briefly while the first lines are prepared so you never miss the start.
  • Standard voices only. VexAI uses ordinary synthetic text-to-speech voices and never clones or imitates a real person's voice.
  • Free voice option. Choose a free voice, or your device's own voices, without spending credits.
  • Many languages. Translate into English, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and more.

Questions

Does every video work?

VexAI needs the video's subtitles to translate. Most YouTube videos have auto-generated captions; videos with no captions at all cannot be translated.

Is it free?

Translated subtitles are free and need no account. The voice-over needs a free Google sign-in; a free voice is available, and premium voices use credits — new accounts get 20 free credits. See pricing.
