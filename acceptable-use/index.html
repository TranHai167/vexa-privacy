Acceptable Use Policy — VexAI

Effective date: 15 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 18 October 2026  ·  Contact: support@pyktools.com

This Acceptable Use Policy explains what you may not do with VexAI — the browser extension and the website at vexai.pyktools.com. It forms part of our Terms of Service. It applies to everything you ask VexAI to produce: translations, answers, rewritten text, and images created with the optional Vibes feature.

1. Content you may not create

You may not use VexAI to generate, request or try to obtain:

  • NSFW, sexually explicit or sexually suggestive content — nudity, sexual acts, sexualized bodies or poses, fetish content, or erotic material of any kind;
  • any sexual content involving minors, or content that sexualizes or endangers children in any way;
  • images of real, identifiable people — celebrities, public figures or private individuals — including likenesses, "photos" or caricatures of a named real person;
  • deepfakes, face swaps or any face manipulation of real people (VexAI offers no such tool and does not accept photos of people for image generation);
  • graphic violence or gore, self-harm promotion, or content that glorifies terrorism or violent extremism;
  • hateful content — hate symbols, or content that attacks people for their race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation;
  • content that harasses, threatens, bullies or defames a person;
  • content that impersonates a person or organization, or is designed to deceive — fake documents, fake reviews, scams or phishing;
  • content that is illegal where you live or that infringes someone else's copyright, trademark or other rights.

2. Activities you may not do

  • Bypass, probe or try to defeat our content safeguards, rate limits or credit system, for example by rewording a refused prompt to sneak it through.
  • Access the Service other than through the extension — automated scripts, scraping, or reverse engineering our servers or APIs.
  • Resell, sublicense or redistribute the Service or its output as a service of your own.
  • Use VexAI to create malware, spam, or content meant to manipulate search engines.
  • Submit AI-assisted work as your own where your school, employer or publisher forbids it — you are responsible for following those rules.

3. How we enforce this policy

  • Automated checks before generation. Every Vibes image prompt is checked before any image is created, by our own filters and by our payment provider's moderation service. Prompts that break this policy are refused, and no credits are charged for a refused prompt. If the check cannot run, generation is refused rather than allowed.
  • AI providers' own rules. The AI models we use apply their own usage policies and may refuse requests as well.
  • Account action. We may suspend or close accounts that break this policy or repeatedly try to get around our safeguards. For serious breaches we may do so without prior notice and report illegal content to the relevant authorities.

4. Reporting abuse

If you see VexAI being used to create content that breaks this policy, email us at support@pyktools.com with as much detail as you can. We review every report.

5. Changes

We may update this policy as the Service changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version; continued use of VexAI after a change means you accept it.
