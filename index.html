Read anything on the web, in your language.

VexAI is a Chrome extension that translates web pages, images, comics and documents, answers questions about the page you are reading, and dubs YouTube videos — right where you are, without copying and pasting.

Free to install. New accounts get 50 free credits.

One assistant for everything you read

Open the panel on any page, pick what you need, and keep reading.

Comics & images

Translate manga, manhwa and manhua pages in place — speech bubbles are replaced with your language, a page or a whole chapter at a time.

Page Assistant

Ask questions about the article you are reading, get summaries and explanations, and optionally search the web for context.

Web page translation

Translate a whole page while keeping its layout, or show the original and the translation side by side. Select any text for an instant translation.

YouTube dubbing

Translated subtitles and natural AI voice-over for YouTube videos, synced with playback.

Snip & translate

Draw a box around any text on screen — in images and videos — and read the translation instantly.

Documents & writing

Translate documents with your preferred tone and terminology, polish your own writing, and check whether a text or image looks AI-generated.
