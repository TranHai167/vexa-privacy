Read anything on the web, in your language.

VexAI is a Chrome extension that translates web pages, images, comics and documents, answers questions about the page you are reading, and dubs YouTube videos — right where you are, without copying and pasting.

One assistant for everything you read

Open the panel on any page, pick what you need, and keep reading.

Comics & images

Translate manga, manhwa and manhua pages in place — speech bubbles are replaced with your language, a page or a whole chapter at a time.

Page Assistant

Ask questions about the article you are reading, get summaries and explanations, and optionally search the web for context.

Web page translation

Translate a whole page while keeping its layout, or show the original and the translation side by side. Select any text for an instant translation.

YouTube dubbing

Translated subtitles and an AI voice-over in standard synthetic voices, synced with playback — no voice cloning.

Snip & translate

Draw a box around any text on screen — in images and videos — and read the translation instantly.

Documents & writing

Translate documents with your preferred tone and terminology, polish or rewrite text so it reads naturally, and check whether a text or image looks AI-generated.

Private by design

VexAI only reads a page when you ask it to. There is no analytics or tracking in the extension, and we never sell your data. Read exactly what is sent where in our Privacy Policy.

A small team, just getting started

VexAI is made by Pyktools, a small and newly founded team, so some features and AI providers are still limited. Your support and feedback mean a lot — tell us what works, what doesn't and what you'd like next at support@pyktools.com, or with the feedback form in the extension (Settings → Support).
