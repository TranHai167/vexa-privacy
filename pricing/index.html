Frequently asked questions

How many credits does a feature use?

It depends on the feature and the amount of content — short actions such as translating a comic page cost little, while long conversations with premium models cost more. Your balance and a full history of every charge are always visible in the extension, and requests that fail are refunded automatically.

When can I buy credit packs?

Soon. Until then, use your 50 free credits and the free daily allowances. We will announce it in the extension when packs are available.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every pack is a one-time purchase. You are never charged again unless you buy another pack.

Who processes my payment?

Purchases are not open yet. When they are, payments will be handled by a third-party payment provider, named at checkout, which also handles sales tax. We will never see or store your card details.

Can I get a refund?

Once packs are on sale: yes, within 14 days of purchase if you have not spent any credits since buying the pack. Free credits cannot be refunded. See the Refund Policy.

Why is the price at checkout higher than shown here?

Prices here exclude tax. The VAT or sales tax that applies in your country will be added at checkout.
