Frequently asked questions

How many credits does a feature use?

It depends on the feature and the amount of content — short actions such as translating a comic page cost little, while long conversations with premium models cost more. Your balance and a full history of every charge are always visible in the extension, and requests that fail are refunded automatically.

How do I buy a credit pack?

Open the extension's Settings → Pricing and choose a pack. A secure checkout window opens; after payment the credits appear in your balance within a few seconds.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every pack is a one-time purchase. You are never charged again unless you buy another pack.

Who processes my payment?

Payments are processed by Creem, our reseller (Merchant of Record), which also handles VAT/sales tax and sends your receipt. Cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay are accepted (the methods offered depend on your country and device). We never see or store your card details.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of purchase if you have not spent any credits since buying the pack. Free credits cannot be refunded. See the Refund Policy.

Why is the price at checkout higher than shown here?

Prices here exclude tax. The VAT or sales tax that applies in your country will be added at checkout.
