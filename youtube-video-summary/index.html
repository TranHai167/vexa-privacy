Summarize YouTube videos in your language

VexAI adds a summary card next to the YouTube player: the key points of the video in a few lines, plus timestamped highlights — click one to jump straight to that moment.

Add to Chrome — it's free

Needs a free Google sign-in.

How it works

  1. Open a YouTube video that has subtitles (manual or auto-generated).
  2. Get the summary. Depending on your setting, the card summarizes the video by itself or when you click the button.
  3. Jump around. Click a highlight's timestamp to go to that part of the video, or pick another language on the card to read the summary in it.

Details that matter

  • Reads the subtitles, not the audio. The extension reads the video's subtitle text in your own browser and sends it to the AI model; nothing is recorded or downloaded.
  • Remembered on your device. Finished summaries are kept in your browser, so reopening a video does not run it again.
  • You decide when. Summarize always, only when you have enough free questions or credits left, or only when you click — or hide the card completely in Settings → YouTube.
  • Many languages. Read summaries in English, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and more.

Questions

Does every video work?

VexAI needs the video's subtitles. Most YouTube videos have auto-generated captions; videos with no captions at all, and ongoing live streams, cannot be summarized.

Is it free?

With the free model, each summary uses one of your free Page Assistant questions while you have some left. With a premium model it uses credits based on the video's length — new accounts get 20 free credits. See pricing.

Where did the translated subtitles and voice-over go?

YouTube subtitle translation and dubbing were retired in version 1.0.4 and replaced by video summaries.
